How I evaluate a model response

The criteria I check

When comparing or ranking outputs, I score each response against the project's defined criteria and write a short justification for the decision.

CriterionWhat I look forTypical flag
Factual accuracyClaims are correct and supported by credible sources.unsupported_claim
RelevanceThe response answers what was actually asked.off_task
CompletenessEvery part of the request is addressed, with no missing context.missing_info
Instruction-followingFormatting rules and user constraints are respected.constraint_violated
ConsistencyNo internal contradictions within the response.contradiction
Reasoning qualityLogic holds up; calculations, units, and assumptions are sound.calc_error
ClarityThe answer is unambiguous and easy to follow.ambiguous

Research & quality workflow

My process on every task

  1. Read everything firstFull instructions, examples, label definitions, and the rubric before any scoring or labeling.
  2. Separate fact from assumptionVerified, source-supported conclusions are kept apart from speculation.
  3. Check the request was metConfirm the output addresses the request and follows every constraint.
  4. Weigh the evidenceAssess source reliability, internal consistency, and completeness.
  5. Write the rationaleExplain concisely why an item is correct, incorrect, incomplete, or ambiguous.
  6. Stay consistent at scaleApply the same judgment across large batches and document edge cases for QA review.
